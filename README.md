# NEREIDA — Seasonal Forecast Portfolio Viewer

An interactive map viewer for NEREIDA's monthly seasonal hydrological
forecasts across the Ter and Tordera basins (Catalonia): streamflow, river
temperature, and Sau–Susqueda reservoir system storage and outlet gate
temperature. Built as a portfolio-style companion to the operational
[`hydro_tool`](https://github.com/NEREIDACA/hydro_tool) viewer, presenting
the same underlying forecasts in a different chart style in English.

## What it shows

A split-screen Leaflet map: click a station, pick a forecast month from the
date selector (or **Latest forecast**), and the right-hand panel loads that
station's seasonal forecast chart for the selected month.

Each chart is a two-panel figure:

- **Top — ensemble spread.** The 51-member SEAS5.1 ensemble's median,
  33–66% and 10–90% envelopes across the 7-month horizon (t+1…t+7), against
  the observed climatological tercile bands for each calendar lead month
  (dashed lines = that month's P33/P66, shaded bands = below/normal/above).
  The observed value is overlaid where available.
- **Bottom — tercile probability.** What share of the ensemble falls below,
  within, or above the climatological normal range at each lead — i.e. the
  operational probability of a below-, normal-, or above-normal outcome.

An "About this pipeline" button in the header opens a panel that documents
the full forecast chain, variable list, chart-reading guide, and verified
skill criterion directly in the page — see **Pipeline details**, below,
for the same content.

Stations and their charts:

- **Ter basin** — Ripoll, Roda de Ter (Sau inflow), Gola del Ter (river
  mouth): discharge and river temperature.
- **Tordera basin** — Sant Celoni, Fogars de la Selva, coastal reach:
  discharge and river temperature.
- **Reservoirs** — Sau (system storage + 3 gates) and Susqueda (4 gates):
  outlet gate temperature.

## Data source and how the charts are built

All content here comes from the same seasonal forecasting pipeline as
`hydro_tool` — [`NEREIDACA/main`](https://github.com/NEREIDACA/main)
(Copernicus SEAS5.1 → GR4J hydrology + GLM 3.1.1/AED2 reservoir model,
calibrated on the 1995–2025 hindcast). Nothing in this viewer runs a model;
it only displays pre-rendered PNG charts.

The charts themselves are generated by
[`codes/generate_all_timeseries_plots.R`](https://github.com/NEREIDACA/main/blob/main/codes/generate_all_timeseries_plots.R)
in the main pipeline repo — a standalone, read-only script that reads the
same forecast ensembles, observed climatology and skill scores as the
existing `generate_all_density_plots.R`, and renders the alternative
two-panel chart style described above. It
does not modify or replace any existing pipeline script or output.

## Pipeline details

The content below is also shown in-page via the **About this pipeline**
button — kept here too so it's readable directly from the repo.

**The forecast chain** (runs unattended on the 15th of every month; every
stage carries all 51 ensemble members forward, so each output is a
probability distribution, never a single number):

1. **Forcing** — SEAS5.1 seasonal ensemble: 51 members from the Copernicus
   Climate Data Store, re-downloaded every month.
2. **Correction** — bias adjustment & downscaling: every member corrected
   against the ERA5 reanalysis record.
3. **Hydrology** — rainfall–runoff over the Ter/Tordera sub-basins (GR4J) →
   inflows and system storage.
4. **Lake model** — GLM 3.1.1 + AED2: 1-D thermal structure and mixing at
   Sau and Susqueda, forced by those inflows.
5. **Product** — published monthly: tercile probabilities and verified
   skill per lead month, pushed to the live viewers.

**What's forecast:**

| Family | Variables |
|---|---|
| Water quantity | System storage (Sau + Susqueda combined volume); inflows at six gauges across the Ter and Tordera |
| Water quality | Outlet gate temperature at seven gates across both reservoirs; river temperature at six reaches; water residence time (AED2 age tracer per gate); saline intrusion risk at 52 coastal wells |

**Verified skill.** Every site is verified against a 31-year hindcast
(1995–2025), averaged across all twelve initialisation months, using CRPSS
(continuous ranked probability skill score, vs. climatology) and NSE
(Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency). Published skill criterion:
`NSE > 0 & CRPSS >= 0.1` — sites that fail it are shown as low- or no-skill
rather than hidden. In practice: system storage is genuinely predictable
(CRPSS 0.86 at t+1, still 0.14 at t+7 — a reservoir integrates its own
history). Inflow skill is positive only at t+1; beyond that, climatology is
as good as the forecast. Gate temperature reproduces the right seasonal
shape but does not yet beat climatology (CRPSS below zero).

## About NEREIDA

Built by Daniel Mercado-Bettín, Rafael Marcé and Jordi Pagès at the Centre d'Estudis
Avançats de Blanes (CEAB–CSIC). Applied here to Sau and Susqueda, the tandem
reservoirs on the River Ter that supply drinking water to the Barcelona
metropolitan area — a system pushed to record lows during the 2021–2024
Catalan drought. Both reservoirs are managed by the Agència Catalana de
l'Aigua (ACA); this work is part of the NEREIDA project, grant
RDI001/24/000044, funded by ACA under the R+D+I call ACC/1362/2024.
